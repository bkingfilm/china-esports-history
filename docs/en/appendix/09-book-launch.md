# The Launch Event for ONE MORE WAY: The History Behind Chinese Esports

> A record of the book's launch event by BBKinG (Liu Yang), author of ONE MORE WAY: The History Behind Chinese Esports, published after the book. Translated from the Chinese original: [《中国电竞幕后史》新书发布会](../../zh/appendix/09-新书发布会.md)
>
> First published in the author's Zhihu column on December 5, 2015: https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/20393445
>
> This is a working translation awaiting review against the Chinese original. If you spot a mistranslation, a wrong name, or a factual error, please [open an issue](https://github.com/bkingfilm/china-esports-history/issues/new) or send a pull request.

By BBKinG

Life is one exam after another, and yesterday I sat another one. It does not matter how well I did, because I found that a whole group of old friends were willing to come and sit it alongside me. That is enough.

On December 4, 2015, I held the launch event for The History Behind Chinese Esports. I am sure I will remember that day for the rest of my life.

Finally, my thanks to Wangyu Internet Café (网鱼网咖), and to Huang Feng in particular. While I was still hesitating over whether to hold a launch event at all, Mr Huang told me he was willing to sponsor it and wanted nothing in return. That gave those of us in esports, many of whom had not seen one another for years, a chance to gather again. My thanks to all these people who work quietly behind the scenes for esports.

When my next book comes out, I hope to do it on a bigger and better scale. Everyone can bring their children along and we will turn it into a family party. Thank you all again for coming. I really did put you to a great deal of trouble.
