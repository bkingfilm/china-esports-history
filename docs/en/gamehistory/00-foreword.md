**Foreword to *Behind the Scenes of Chinese Gaming History***

By BBKinG

In 2013, during the Chinese New Year holiday at home, restless with spare time, I began drafting *Behind the Scenes of Chinese Esports History*. My initial intention was modest: merely to cast a brick to attract jade—to document our youth and create a shared archive for those within the esports industry.

When I had completed roughly ten chapters, a reader left a comment that read: "What you are writing is a microcosm of an era; it also marks the formation of esports culture."

Those words prompted me to re-evaluate my work and instilled in me a profound sense of mission.

Consequently, I significantly expanded the scope of my character interviews. Seizing the opportunity of business trips across China, I met with peers and senior figures from all corners of the country.

Now, a year and a half has passed. I have interviewed over fifty industry insiders, producing more than 300,000 Chinese characters that cover representatives from every facet of esports: media outlets, practitioners, casters, players, game operators, developers, club managers, sponsors, hardware manufacturers, and league organizers. Through these conversations, I uncovered many behind-the-scenes stories from deep within the industry—insider experiences I had only heard whispers of but never witnessed firsthand.

The more stories I gathered, the more I realized how deeply Chinese esports is intertwined with the broader history of Chinese gaming. Many key figures in today’s gaming companies were originally rooted in esports; they spent years fighting on the front lines of the industry with an esports mindset before bringing fresh perspectives back to the field. Had these narratives been confined within a strictly esports-centric framework, their full scope and depth could never have been fully presented.
A new thought struck me: why not expand this behind-the-scenes narrative into the broader realm of gaming? I wished to trace the threads of Chinese gaming history, thereby offering esports a wider perspective and richer reference points.

Thus was born our new column, *Behind the Scenes of Chinese Gaming History*.

I will not pretend to know how far this endeavor will reach; much like my initial foray into chronicling the history of Chinese esports, I remain uncertain of the journey’s end, nor can I deny that errors and omissions are inevitable. Yet, with the hope that my humble efforts might inspire more profound contributions, and driven by a desire to preserve the memories of our youth, I look forward to sharing new stories and embarking on this journey together.

To the veterans of the industry and my dear readers, simply call me Xiao Liu... ^^
